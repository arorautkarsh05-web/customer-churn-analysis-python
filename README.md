# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges for telecom companies. This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a customer churn dataset to identify the major factors influencing customer attrition.

The analysis uses Python and popular data analysis libraries to clean, visualize, and extract meaningful business insights from the dataset.

---

## 🎯 Objective

- Understand customer churn behavior.
- Identify factors affecting customer retention.
- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Generate business insights through visualizations.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains customer demographic details, subscription information, billing information, contract type, internet services, payment methods, satisfaction scores, and churn status.

**Dataset Features Include:**

- Customer Demographics
- Gender
- Age
- Contract Type
- Internet Service
- Payment Method
- Monthly Charges
- Total Revenue
- Tenure
- Satisfaction Score
- Churn Label

## 📊 Key Insights

### Customer Churn

- Around 27% customers churned while most customers stayed.

### Contract Type

- Month-to-Month customers have the highest churn.
- Two-Year contract customers have the lowest churn.

### Customer Satisfaction

- Lower satisfaction scores strongly increase churn probability.

### Monthly Charges

- Customers with higher monthly charges are more likely to churn.

### Tenure

- Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to leave.

### Revenue

- Retained customers generate significantly higher revenue.

### Internet Service

- Customers using internet services contribute to most churn cases.

### Gender

- Gender has very little impact on customer churn.

---

## 📌 Business Recommendations

- Encourage customers to switch to long-term contracts.
- Improve customer satisfaction through better service.
- Offer discounts to high-risk customers.
- Monitor customers with high monthly charges.
- Provide personalized retention offers for new customers.
